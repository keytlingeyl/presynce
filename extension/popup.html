Attendance Tracker for Google Meet

How to use Presynce

  1. Open Google Meet and join or start your meeting.
  2. Once you are inside the meeting, look for the blue Track Attendance button at the top center of the Google Meet screen.
  3. Click Track Attendance to start tracking participant attendance.
  4. Keep tracking active while your meeting is ongoing. Presynce will continue tracking as long as there is an active internet connection.
  5. When the meeting is finished, click Track Attendance again to stop tracking and generate your attendance record.
Important: Keep your internet connection active while attendance is being tracked to ensure participant activity is recorded properly.
