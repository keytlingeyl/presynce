What Presynce does

Presynce is made of two parts, together called the "Service":

  • a Chrome extension that, when you start tracking in a Google Meet session, reads the participant list and records who was present, when they joined, and for how long; and
  • a web dashboard where you standardize names ("Last Name, First Name M."), review and rename records, organize them into groups, view longitudinal attendance matrices, find absentees, apply optional attendance rules, export to CSV or PDF, and optionally sign in with Google to back up and restore your data.

Presynce is an aid for recording attendance. It does not verify who a participant really is, and it is not a substitute for your own judgment or your institution's official procedures.

Accuracy of records

  • changes to the Google Meet layout that can temporarily stop the extension from reading the participants list; and
  • automatic name formatting, which follows general rules and may format some names differently from how the person writes them. Please check unusual names.
