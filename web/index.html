Step 01

Track Live in Google Meet

Start Presynce during any Google Meet call using the Track Attendance button. The extension records participants and tracks their attendance duration while the meeting is in progress.

If you don’t see the Track Attendance button, try opening the Participants panel in Google Meet or refresh.

Step 02

Clean and Standardize Names

Presynce automatically reformats participant display names into a consistent Last Name, First Name M. format.

Step 03

Organize Attendance by Group

Tag attendance records into group folder by department, class, subject, section, year level, topic, and more. Open any folder to instantly view a consolidated attendance matrix at a glance.

Step 04

Review, Organize, and Export

Use the Session Viewer to review attendance, configure attendance rules, and identify absentees for individual sessions. Export session reports to Excel or PDF, or use the Matrix Viewer to consolidate multiple sessions into an organized attendance matrix and export consolidated records.
